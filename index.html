FAQ

Questions

  • What is it?

    A hemp rolling tray with a built-in ash tray. It has a wide, flat rolling area with raised walls, plus two round wells: one is the ash tray, and the other is a hemp well so your hemp stays out of the way while you roll. The edgeless design has no corners or seams, so it tips clean.

  • What colors can I get?

    Purple Galaxy is in stock and ships in 1 to 3 days, or is ready for local pickup in Austin. Glow Green, Black, White, and Matte Navy are made to order and ship in about 5 to 7 days.

  • How do I pay?

    Pay with Cash App to $movecash2020. In the payment note, include the color, your name, and either your shipping address or the word “pickup.” Cash or Cash App also works at pickup.

  • How does pickup work?

    Local pickup in Austin is $25. After you order, we’ll arrange a public meeting spot in Austin. You can pay with Cash App ahead of time, or with cash or Cash App when we meet.

  • Where do you ship, and how long does it take?

    We ship to the lower 48 US states only, for $34 total ($25 plus $9 flat shipping). We don’t ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or internationally. Purple Galaxy ships in 1 to 3 days. Made-to-order colors ship in about 5 to 7 days.

  • What’s your returns policy?

    If a tray arrives damaged or misprinted, reach out the same way you ordered within 7 days of delivery and include a photo. We’ll send a replacement or a full refund, your choice. Unused Purple Galaxy trays can be returned within 14 days. Made-to-order colors are final sale unless they arrive damaged or defective. See the full policy below.

  • Is there an age requirement?

    Yes. Lomaferro trays are sold to adults 21+ only, for use with legal tobacco and herbal products. By ordering, you confirm you’re 21 or older.
