Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026. This policy describes how Abram Torres, doing business as Lomaferro (“we,” “us”), handles information when you order a tray or contact us. Questions: wqq9zhc9@mail.grokbot.com.

What we collect and why

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name, so we can match the order and the Cash App payment note.
  • Your email address, which is required so we can send USPS tracking updates and reach you about your order.
  • Your shipping address (street, city, state, and ZIP), so we can ship your order to the lower 48 US states.

We do not collect a phone number. We do not require an account to order.

If you email us, we also receive the content of your message, including any photos you send for a damage or return claim.

Payment is made through Cash App. We may see the payment amount and any note you include. We do not receive or store your Cash App login or your card number. Cash App handles that payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Our website host may automatically collect ordinary technical information, such as IP address and browser type, as part of operating the site. We do not use that information to build advertising profiles.

How we use it

We use this information to accept and fulfill orders, ship to you, send tracking updates, process Cash App refunds, respond to questions and claims, keep business records, and comply with tax and legal duties.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

We share information only as needed to complete your order or as required by law: for example, with the carrier that delivers your package, with Cash App for payment and refunds, or with a tax authority or other official if we are legally required to do so.

Retention

We keep order, shipping, and message records as long as needed to fulfill orders and meet tax and legal record requirements, generally up to 4 years, then delete or de-identify them when we no longer need them.
